Hi! I am an undergraduate researcher majoring in Computer Science and Biomedical Engineering at [Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST)](https://www.gist.ac.kr/en/main.html). I am currently working with [Prof. Euiheon Chung](https://gistnpl.github.io/gist-neurophotonicslab/) at GIST and [Prof. Piantadosi](https://colala.berkeley.edu/) at UC Berkeley.

My interest focuses on developing **mathematical tools to enable cognitive interpretation of neural manifolds**. I am particularly interested in how dynamical features change as one develops generalized and abstract cognitive abilities, and what each of these changes means for cognition.

*I'm currently preparing to apply for PhD programs! I'd love to connect with others who are on the same path :)*
